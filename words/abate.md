### Abate (v.), Abatement (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To lessen in force or intensity.
- To reduce in amount or size.
- To lower in price or value.
- (law) To make a writ<sup>1</sup> void; to nullify<sup>2</sup>.
- (law) To put an end to a nuisance<sup>3</sup>.
- To bring down someone mentally or physically.
- To omit or remove (a part from a whole).
- (n.) the act of abating; a reduction.

#### Example:
- The storm began to *abate* after midnight, and the wind dropped.
- As the flood waters *abated*, families returned to their homes.
- We will *abate* this price from the total.
- (law) The writ<sup>1</sup> has *abated*.
- (law) She was ordered by the court to *abate* the nuisance<sup>3</sup>.
- Months in the hospital had *abated* his strength and his spirits.
- The committee agreed to *abate* the disputed clause from the final agreement.
- (n.) The city offered a tax *abatement* to new businesses.

##### Unknown words:
1. Writ - A formal written order from a court.
2. Nullify - To cancel something so it has no effect.
3. Nuisance - Something or someone that annoys you or causes trouble (annoyance, inconvenience).
