### Aberrant (adj./n.)
#### Meaning:
- Differing from the norm.
- Straying<sup>1</sup> from the right way.
- (biology) Deviating<sup>2</sup> from the ordinary or natural type; exceptional; abnormal.
- (n.) A person or thing that deviates<sup>2</sup> from the rest of its group.
- Synonyms: Deviant.

#### Example:
- The lab checked every *aberrant* result twice.
- The doctor noticed an *aberrant* heartbeat on the monitor.
- The board punished the manager for his *aberrant* conduct, which broke every rule of honesty.
- Scientists found an *aberrant* frog with five legs.
- (n.) Among a hundred normal readings, the one very high number was an *aberrant*.
- (Deviant) At the trial, the extent of his *deviant*<sup>3</sup> behavior became clear.
- (Deviant) As the graph shows, the March sales trend is the *deviant*<sup>3</sup>.

##### Unknown words:
1. Stray/Straying - To wander away from the right path or place.
2. Deviate - To turn away from the usual or right path.
3. Deviant - Someone or something that is different from what is normal; often used in a negative way for a person.
