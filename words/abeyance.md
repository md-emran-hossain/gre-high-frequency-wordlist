### Abeyance (n.)
#### Meaning:
- A temporary stop; action that is put on hold.
- (law) Expectancy<sup>1</sup>; a condition of being undetermined, such as a lapse<sup>2</sup> in who owns an estate<sup>3</sup> or title.

#### Example:
- The plan was kept in *abeyance* until the funding was approved.
- The old rule has fallen into *abeyance*; nobody follows it anymore.
- (law) The proceeds<sup>4</sup> of the estate<sup>3</sup> shall be held in *abeyance* in an escrow<sup>5</sup> account until the minor reaches age twenty-one.

##### Unknown words:
1. Expectancy - The state of expecting something.
2. Lapse - A temporary failure; a slip (ex: memory lapse, lapse in security, lapse in concentration).
3. Estate - All the money and property a person owns, especially what is left after death.
4. Proceeds - Money received from a sale or an activity.
5. Escrow - Money or property held by a third party until a condition is met.
