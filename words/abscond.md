### Abscond (v.)
#### Meaning:
- To leave quickly and secretly to avoid being caught.
- (law) To hide or leave to avoid a court case or a debt (for example, while out on bail<sup>1</sup>).
- To run away with money or things that are not yours.

#### Example:
- The prisoner *absconded* from the work camp during the night.
- (law) A man on bail<sup>1</sup> *absconded* and missed his trial.
- The cashier *absconded* with the day's money.

##### Unknown words:
1. Bail - Money paid so a person can wait outside prison before their trial.
