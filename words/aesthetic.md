### Aesthetic (adj./n.)
#### Meaning:
- Connected with beauty and art.
- Pleasing to look at.
- (n.) A style or set of ideas about beauty that belongs to an artist, group or time.

#### Example:
- The architect cared more about *aesthetic* value than cost.
- The new bridge is *aesthetically* pleasing.
- (n.) The film has a dark, gloomy *aesthetic*.
