### Aggregate (v./n./adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To gather things together into one group or total.
- (n.) A total made of many smaller parts.
- (n.) Sand or small stones mixed with cement to make concrete.
- (adj.) Total; taken all together.

#### Example:
- (v.) The website *aggregates* news from many sources.
- (n.) The *aggregate* of their savings was enough to buy a house.
- (n.) Workers mixed cement with *aggregate* to make concrete.
- (adj.) The team won on *aggregate* score after two matches.
