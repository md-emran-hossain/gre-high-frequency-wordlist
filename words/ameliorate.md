### Ameliorate (v.)
#### Meaning:
- To make a bad situation better; to improve it.
- To become better.

#### Example:
- New safety laws aimed to *ameliorate* working conditions in the factories.
- Rain helped to *ameliorate* the drought<sup>1</sup>.
- Her mood *ameliorated* after she heard the good news.
- His symptoms began to *ameliorate* after a week of treatment.

##### Unknown words:
1. Drought - A long period with very little rain.
