### Approbation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Official or formal approval.
- Praise or a good opinion shown to someone.

#### Example:
- The plan received the *approbation* of the city council.
- He sought<sup>1</sup> his father's *approbation* all his life.

##### Unknown words:
1. Sought - (simple past or past participle of *seek*) To try; to find; to look for; to search for; to ask for; to try to acquire or gain.
