### Appropriate (v./adj.), Appropriation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To take something for your own use, often without permission.
- (v.) To set aside money for a special purpose.
- (adj.) Suitable or right for a certain situation.

#### Example:
- (v.) The band was criticized for *appropriating* another culture's music.
- (v.) Parliament *appropriated* funds for new schools.
- (adj.) Formal clothes are *appropriate* for a job interview.
