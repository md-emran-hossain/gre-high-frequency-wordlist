### Austere (adj.), Austerity (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Strict, serious and unfriendly in manner.
- Very plain, with no decoration or comfort.
- (n.) A government policy of cutting spending.

#### Example:
- The *austere* teacher never smiled in class.
- The church was *austere*, with bare white walls.
- (n.) The government introduced *austerity* measures and cut public jobs.
