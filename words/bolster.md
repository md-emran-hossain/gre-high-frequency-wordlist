### Bolster (v./n.)
#### Meaning:
- To make something stronger or give it more support.
- To improve a feeling, such as confidence.
- (n.) A long, firm pillow.

#### Example:
- The bank *bolstered* its savings to be ready for a bad year.
- Winning the first game *bolstered* the team's confidence.
- (n.) She put a *bolster* behind her back on the sofa.
