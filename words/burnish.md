### Burnish (v./n.)
#### Meaning:
- To make a surface shiny by rubbing it.
- To improve something, such as a reputation.
- (n.) A shine on a surface.

#### Example:
- He *burnished* the old brass lamp until it glowed.
- The mayor hoped the festival would *burnish* the city's image.
- (n.) The oak table had a deep *burnish* from years of polishing.
