### Catalyst (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (chemistry) A substance that makes a chemical reaction faster without being used up.
- A person or event that causes change.

#### Example:
- (chemistry) A metal *catalyst* in the car's exhaust system cleans harmful gases.
- The scandal was the *catalyst* for new laws on lobbying<sup>1</sup>.

##### Unknown words:
1. Lobbying - Trying to influence lawmakers on behalf of a group or business.
