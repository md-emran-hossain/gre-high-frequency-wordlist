### Caustic (adj.), Caustic (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Able to burn or destroy things by chemical action.
- (adj.) Bitter and cutting in speech.
- (n.) A strong chemical that burns, such as lye<sup>1</sup>.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Wear gloves when cleaning with *caustic* soda.
- (adj.) The critic's *caustic* review called the play a waste of time.
- (n.) Workers must handle the *caustic* with great care.

##### Unknown words:
1. Lye - A strong chemical used to make soap and clean drains.
