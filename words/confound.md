### Confound (v.)
#### Meaning:
- To confuse or surprise someone so they cannot think clearly.
- To go against what people expected or to spoil a plan.
- To mix up one thing with another.

#### Example:
- The strange question *confounded* the whole class.
- The team *confounded* the experts and won the title.
- Do not *confound* a cold with the flu; they are different illnesses.
