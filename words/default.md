### Default (n.), Default (v.), Default (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Failure to pay a debt on time.
- (n.) The setting that is used automatically when you choose nothing.
- (v.) To fail to pay a debt or keep an agreement.
- (adj.) Automatic; used when no other choice is made.

#### Example:
- (n.) The company is in *default* on its bank loan.
- (n.) The phone's *default* language is English.
- (v.) Many families *defaulted* on their mortgages<sup>1</sup> during the crisis.
- (adj.) The *default* font in this program is Arial.

##### Unknown words:
1. Mortgage - A loan from a bank to buy a house.
