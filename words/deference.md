### Deference (n.), Defer (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Polite respect for someone's opinion or position, shown by accepting what they want.
- (v.) To delay something until a later time.

#### Example:
- (n.) Out of *deference* to the family, the shop stayed closed on the day of the funeral.
- (v.) The board *deferred* the vote until next month.
