### Derivative (adj.), Derivative (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Copied from or based on something else; not original.
- (n.) A word formed from another word.
- (math) A measure of how fast a value changes.
- (finance) A contract whose value depends on another asset<sup>1</sup>.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Reviewers called the film *derivative* because it copied older movies.
- (n.) "Happiness" is a *derivative* of "happy".
- (math) The *derivative* of a car's position is its speed.
- (finance) Banks lost billions on risky *derivatives* in 2008.

##### Unknown words:
1. Asset - Something of value that a person or company owns.
