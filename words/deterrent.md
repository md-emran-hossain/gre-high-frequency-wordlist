### Deterrent (n.), Deterrent (adj.), Deter (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Something that makes people not want to do something, often from fear of the result.
- (adj.) Able to stop or discourage.

#### Example:
- (n.) Cameras in the shop are a *deterrent* to thieves.
- (adj.) The *deterrent* effect of a big fine made drivers slow down.
