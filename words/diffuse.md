### Diffuse (adj.), Diffuse (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Spread out over a wide area, not in one place.
- (adj.) Using too many words; not clear or direct.
- (v.) To spread out in all directions, like gas, light or ideas.

#### Example:
- (adj.) A *diffuse* light came through the cloudy sky.
- (adj.) The essay was *diffuse* and hard to follow.
- (v.) The smell of coffee *diffused* through the whole house.
