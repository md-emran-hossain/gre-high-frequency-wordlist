### Discerning (adj.), Discern (v.), Discernment (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Good at judging quality; able to see small differences and choose well.
- (v.) To notice or recognize something that is hard to see or understand.

#### Example:
- (adj.) *Discerning* shoppers compare prices before buying.
- (v.) In the fog, I could barely *discern* the shape of the boat.
