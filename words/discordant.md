### Discordant (adj.), Discord (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Not agreeing; not fitting together.
- (adj.) Sounding harsh because the notes clash.
- (n.) Disagreement and quarrels among people.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The two witnesses gave *discordant* accounts of the accident.
- (adj.) A *discordant* note from the violin ruined the end of the concert.
- (n.) Family *discord* over the will kept the relatives from speaking.
