### Discredit (v.), Discredit (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To make people stop trusting or respecting someone.
- (v.) To show that an idea or evidence is false or not reliable.
- (n.) Loss of trust or good name.
- (n.) A person or thing that brings shame on a group.

#### Example:
- (v.) The tax scandal *discredited* the minister, and he resigned.
- (v.) New test results *discredited* the old theory about the disease.
- (n.) After the fraud, the company fell into *discredit* with investors.
- (n.) A coach who cheats is a *discredit* to the whole sport.
