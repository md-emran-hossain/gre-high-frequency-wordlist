### Disinterested (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Fair, because you have nothing to gain or lose from the result.
- Not interested (common in casual speech, but some people say it is wrong).

#### Example:
- A judge must be *disinterested* and must not know the people in the case.
- We hired a *disinterested* expert to check the safety report.
- She seemed *disinterested* in the lesson and looked out of the window.
