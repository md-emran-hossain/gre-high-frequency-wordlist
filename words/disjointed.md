### Disjointed (adj.), Disjoint (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Not connected in a clear order; hard to follow.
- (v.) To cut or take apart at the joints.

#### Example:
- (adj.) His speech was *disjointed*, and the audience lost the main point.
- (adj.) Critics said the film's *disjointed* plot jumped between years without warning.
- (v.) The cook *disjointed* the chicken with a sharp knife before frying it.
