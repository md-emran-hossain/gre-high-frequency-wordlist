### Dismiss (v.)
#### Meaning:
- To refuse to take something seriously; to reject it.
- To tell someone they may leave.
- To remove someone from a job; to fire.
- (law) To end a court case without a full trial.
- (cricket) To get a batter out.

#### Example:
- Do not *dismiss* her warning just because she is young.
- The teacher *dismissed* the class five minutes early.
- The firm *dismissed* two workers for stealing.
- The judge *dismissed* the case because there was no evidence.
- The bowler *dismissed* three batters in one over.
