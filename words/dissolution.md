### Dissolution (n.)
#### Meaning:
- The formal ending of a group, marriage, or agreement.
- The breaking down of something into small parts.
- Life with bad habits and loose morals (old use).

#### Example:
- The *dissolution* of parliament led to a new election.
- The *dissolution* of the old company took two years.
- The novel describes a rich man's *dissolution* and his slow ruin.
