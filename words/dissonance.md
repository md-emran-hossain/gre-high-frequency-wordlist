### Dissonance (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Sounds that clash and are unpleasant together.
- A lack of agreement between ideas, beliefs, or actions.

#### Example:
- The song ends with a harsh *dissonance* that makes the listener uneasy.
- There was *dissonance* between the manager's kind words and her cold actions.
- Eating meat while loving animals can cause cognitive *dissonance*.
