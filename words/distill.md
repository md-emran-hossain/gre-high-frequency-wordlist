### Distill (v.)
#### Meaning:
- To purify a liquid by heating it to vapor and cooling it again.
- To make strong alcohol this way.
- To pull out the most important part of a large amount of information.

#### Example:
- Some islands *distill* seawater to get drinking water.
- The farm *distills* its own whiskey and sells it locally.
- She *distilled* a forty-page report into one page for the boss.
