### Document (n.), Document (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) An official paper or file with written information.
- (n.) A computer file of text.
- (v.) To record something in writing, photos, or video as proof.

#### Example:
- (n.) Please sign the *document* at the bottom and return it to the bank.
- (n.) Save the *document* before you close the program.
- (v.) The reporter *documented* the flood damage with photos and videos.
