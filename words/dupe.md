### Dupe (n.), Dupe (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person who is tricked or used by others without knowing it.
- (v.) To trick someone into believing something false.

#### Example:
- (n.) He was the *dupe* of a clever thief and lost all his savings.
- (v.) Sellers *duped* tourists by selling them fake gold.
- (v.) Do not be *duped* by ads that promise quick money.
