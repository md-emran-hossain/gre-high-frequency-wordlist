### Eclectic (adj.), Eclectic (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Using ideas, styles, or things from many different sources.
- (n.) A person who chooses from many sources.

#### Example:
- (adj.) She has an *eclectic* taste in music, from jazz to folk to rock.
- (adj.) The cafe has *eclectic* furniture from many eras.
- (n.) As an *eclectic*, he mixes cooking styles from five countries.
