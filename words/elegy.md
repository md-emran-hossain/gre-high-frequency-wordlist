### Elegy (n.), Elegiac (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A sad poem or song, especially for someone who died.
- (n.) A sad piece of writing or film about something lost.
- (adj.) Sad and thoughtful, like an elegy.

#### Example:
- (n.) The poet read an *elegy* for his dead friend at the service.
- (n.) The film is an *elegy* to a fishing village that no longer exists.
- (adj.) The novel has an *elegiac* tone about childhood summers.
