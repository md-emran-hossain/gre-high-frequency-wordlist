### Endemic (adj.), Endemic (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Found regularly in one place or group of people.
- (adj.) Native to one area only, as a plant or animal.
- (n.) A disease that is always present in a region.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Malaria is *endemic* in parts of Africa.
- (adj.) The lemur is *endemic* to Madagascar and lives nowhere else.
- (n.) Doctors now treat the flu as an *endemic*, not an epidemic<sup>1</sup>.

##### Unknown words:
1. Epidemic - A sudden outbreak of disease that spreads fast.
