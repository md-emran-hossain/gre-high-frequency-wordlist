### Enervate (v.), Enervating (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To take away strength or energy; to make weak and tired.
- (adj.) Making you feel weak and tired.

#### Example:
- (v.) The heat *enervated* the workers by noon.
- (v.) A long illness *enervated* her for months.
- (adj.) The *enervating* humidity made work hard.
