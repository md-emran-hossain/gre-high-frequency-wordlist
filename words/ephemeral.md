### Ephemeral (adj.), Ephemeral (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Lasting a very short time.
- (n.) Something that lasts a very short time, such as a plant or insect that lives one day.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Internet fame can be *ephemeral*.
- (adj.) Snow is *ephemeral* in this warm city.
- (n.) The desert flowers are *ephemerals* that bloom for one week after rain.
