### Extrapolation (n.), Extrapolate (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A guess about the unknown made by continuing a known pattern.
- (v.) To estimate something by extending known facts.

#### Example:
- (n.) The sales forecast is an *extrapolation* from the last three years.
- (v.) We can *extrapolate* next year's growth from this graph.
- (v.) It is risky to *extrapolate* from only two cases.
