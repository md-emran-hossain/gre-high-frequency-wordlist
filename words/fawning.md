### Fawning (adj.), Fawn (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Trying to win favor with too much praise and flattery<sup>1</sup>.
- (v.) To behave this way toward someone powerful.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The *fawning* assistant laughed at all of the boss's jokes.
- (adj.) Reporters should not write *fawning* stories about politicians.
- (v.) Fans *fawned* over the singer at the party.

##### Unknown words:
1. Flattery - Too much praise, often not honest.
