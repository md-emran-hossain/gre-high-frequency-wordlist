### Flag (n.), Flag (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A piece of cloth with colors or a design, used as a sign of a country or group.
- (n.) A mark on something that shows it needs attention.
- (v.) To lose energy or interest; to become weak.
- (v.) To mark something so it gets noticed.
- (v.) To signal a vehicle to stop by waving.

#### Example:
- (n.) The national *flag* was raised at dawn.
- (n.) A red *flag* next to an email means it is urgent.
- (v.) By the end of the long hike, our energy began to *flag*.
- (v.) The bank's system *flagged* the payment as suspicious.
- (v.) She stepped into the road to *flag* down a taxi.
