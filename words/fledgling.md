### Fledgling (adj.), Fledgling (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) New and without experience; just starting.
- (n.) A young bird that has just grown its flying feathers.
- (n.) A person or group that is new and still learning.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The *fledgling* company had only three workers.
- (n.) The *fledgling* fell from the nest but soon learned to fly.
- (n.) As a *fledgling* in politics, she needed good advisers.
