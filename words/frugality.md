### Frugality (n.), Frugal (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Careful use of money; spending little and wasting nothing.
- (adj.) Careful with money; simple and cheap.

#### Example:
- (n.) Their *frugality* let them retire early.
- (adj.) He is *frugal*, so he rarely eats out.
- (adj.) They ate a *frugal* dinner of bread and soup.
