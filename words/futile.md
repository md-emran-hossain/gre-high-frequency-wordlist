### Futile (adj.), Futility (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Having no chance of success; pointless.
- (n.) The fact that something is pointless.

#### Example:
- (adj.) It is *futile* to argue with him when he is angry.
- (adj.) Their *futile* efforts to save the old building failed.
- (n.) She felt the *futility* of searching in the dark.
