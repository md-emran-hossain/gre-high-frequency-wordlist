### Goad (v.), Goad (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To push or annoy someone until they do something.
- (n.) Anything that pushes a person to act.

#### Example:
- (v.) His friends *goaded* him into jumping off the rock.
- (v.) She would not be *goaded* into an argument.
- (n.) Fear of failure was a constant *goad* to her.
