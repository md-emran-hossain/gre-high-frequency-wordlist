### Gouge (v.), Gouge (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To cut or dig out something roughly, making a hole or groove<sup>1</sup>.
- (v.) To charge too much money when people have no choice (informal).
- (n.) A deep, rough cut or hole.
- (n.) A curved chisel<sup>2</sup> for cutting wood.

#### Example:
- (v.) The rock *gouged* a deep hole in the boat.
- (v.) Shops *gouge* customers after a storm by raising the price of water.
- (n.) There was a long *gouge* in the table where it was dragged.
- (n.) The carver used a *gouge* to shape the bowl.

##### Unknown words:
1. Groove - A long narrow cut in a surface.
2. Chisel - A tool with a sharp end for cutting wood or stone.
