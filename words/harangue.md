### Harangue (n.), Harangue (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A long, loud, angry speech that scolds or tries to persuade.
- (v.) To give such a speech to someone.

#### Example:
- (n.) The coach's *harangue* after the loss lasted twenty minutes.
- (v.) A man *harangued* the crowd about taxes.
- (v.) She *harangued* her son for coming home late.
