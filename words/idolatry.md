### Idolatry (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Worship of statues or images as gods.
- Too great love or admiration for a person or thing.

#### Example:
- Ancient prophets<sup>1</sup> spoke strongly against *idolatry*.
- Fans' *idolatry* of the singer went too far.

##### Unknown words:
1. Prophet - A person who speaks for God.
