### Impede (v.), Impediment (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To slow down or block someone or something.
- (n.) Something that blocks or slows progress.
- (n.) A speech problem such as a stammer.

#### Example:
- (v.) Fallen trees *impeded* the rescue team.
- (n.) Lack of money was the main *impediment* to the project.
- (n.) He has a speech *impediment*, but he is a fine teacher.
