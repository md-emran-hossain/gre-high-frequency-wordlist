### Implacable (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Not able to be calmed or made to forgive.
- Impossible to stop; relentless<sup>1</sup>.

#### Example:
- He was an *implacable* enemy who never forgave.
- She faced *implacable* opposition from the committee.

##### Unknown words:
1. Relentless - Never stopping or getting weaker.
