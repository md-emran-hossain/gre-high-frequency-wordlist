### Implode (v.), Implosion (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To collapse inward suddenly and with great force.
- (v.) To fail suddenly and completely (a company, plan, or team).
- (n.) Implosion: a sudden collapse inward, or a sudden total failure.

#### Example:
- (v.) The old stadium *imploded* in seconds when workers set off the charges.
- (v.) The startup *imploded* after its founder was arrested for fraud.
- (n.) The *implosion* of the team's season left fans angry.
