### Inadvertently (adv.), Inadvertent (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (adv.) Without meaning to; by accident or oversight.
- (adj.) Inadvertent: not done on purpose.

#### Example:
- (adv.) I *inadvertently* deleted the file instead of saving it.
- (adv.) The company *inadvertently* sent the customer list to the wrong email address.
- (adj.) The error was *inadvertent*, and the bank apologized.
