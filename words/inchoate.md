### Inchoate (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Just begun and not yet fully formed.
- Confused and not clearly organized.
- (law) Describing a crime that was started or planned but not completed, such as an attempt.

#### Example:
- She had only an *inchoate* idea for her novel, with no plot yet.
- The protest was still *inchoate*, with no leader and no clear demands.
- (law) Conspiracy is an *inchoate* crime, because the planned act may never happen.
