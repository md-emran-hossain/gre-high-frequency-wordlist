### Incongruity (n.), Incongruous (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Something that does not fit or match its surroundings.
- (n.) A lack of agreement between two things; oddness.
- (adj.) Incongruous: out of place; not fitting.

#### Example:
- (n.) The *incongruity* of a bright pink car at a funeral made some guests smile.
- (n.) Reporters noted the *incongruity* between the mayor's promises and his actions.
- (adj.) A modern glass tower looked *incongruous* beside the old stone church.
