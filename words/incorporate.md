### Incorporate (v.), Incorporated (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To include something as a part of a larger whole.
- (cooking) To mix an ingredient fully into another.
- (law) To form a company as a legal body<sup>1</sup>.
- (adj.) Incorporated: legally formed as a company (Inc.).

#### Example:
- (v.) The designer will *incorporate* your feedback into the final plan.
- (cooking) *Incorporate* the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each.
- (law) The two friends *incorporated* their bakery in 2019 to protect their savings.
- (adj.) She works for an *incorporated* firm, so its name ends in "Inc."

##### Unknown words:
1. Legal body - An organization the law treats as one person (it can own things and sign contracts).
