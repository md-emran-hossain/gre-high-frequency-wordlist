### Indeterminate (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Not clearly known or decided.
- Not exact; vague.
- With no fixed end, such as a contract or a prison sentence.

#### Example:
- The flight was delayed for an *indeterminate* time.
- He wore a coat of *indeterminate* color, somewhere between gray and green.
- The judge gave him an *indeterminate* sentence, so his release date is not set.
