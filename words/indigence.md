### Indigence (n.), Indigent (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Extreme poverty; not having enough money for basic needs.
- (adj.) Indigent: very poor. Also used as a noun for a very poor person.

#### Example:
- (n.) Many families fell into *indigence* after the factory closed.
- (adj.) The court gives a free lawyer to any *indigent* defendant.
