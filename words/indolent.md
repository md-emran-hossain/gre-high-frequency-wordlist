### Indolent (adj.), Indolence (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Lazy; liking to avoid work.
- (medicine) Growing or healing slowly and causing little pain.

#### Example:
- The *indolent* student never turned in his homework.
- Her *indolence* annoyed her busy coworkers.
- (medicine) The doctor found an *indolent* ulcer<sup>1</sup> on his leg that had not healed for months.

##### Unknown words:
1. Ulcer - An open sore on the skin or inside the body.
