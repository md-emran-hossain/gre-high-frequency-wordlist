### Inert (adj.), Inertia (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Not moving or not able to move.
- (adj.) Slow and without energy.
- (chemistry) Not reacting with other substances.
- (n.) Inertia: the tendency to stay as you are, either still or moving; unwillingness to change.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The rescuers found the climber *inert* in the snow.
- (adj.) After a big lunch he felt *inert* and sleepy at his desk.
- (chemistry) Helium is an *inert* gas, so it is safe to use in balloons.
- (n.) Bureaucratic *inertia* slowed every change at the agency.
