### Innocuous (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Not harmful or dangerous.
- Not likely to upset anyone; mild or dull.

#### Example:
- The snake is *innocuous*; it has no venom<sup>1</sup>.
- It seemed an *innocuous* question, but he got angry.

##### Unknown words:
1. Venom - Poison that an animal puts into a victim by biting or stinging.
