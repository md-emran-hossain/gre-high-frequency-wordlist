### Insensible (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Unconscious; not awake.
- Not able to feel pain, cold, or touch.
- Not aware of something, or not caring about it.

#### Example:
- The boxer was knocked *insensible* by one punch.
- After an hour in the snow, her fingers were *insensible* to the cold.
- The manager seemed *insensible* to the workers' anger.
