### Insinuate (v.), Insinuation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To suggest something bad in an indirect way, without saying it openly.
- (v.) To slowly and cleverly work your way into a place or someone's favor.
- (n.) Insinuation: an indirect, often unkind suggestion.

#### Example:
- (v.) Are you *insinuating* that I took the money?
- (v.) He *insinuated* himself into the director's circle of friends to get promoted.
- (n.) I resent the *insinuation* in your email that I was late on purpose.
