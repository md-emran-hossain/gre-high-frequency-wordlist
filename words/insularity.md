### Insularity (n.), Insular (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Narrow thinking; lack of interest in other people's ideas or cultures.
- (n.) The state of being cut off from others, like an island.
- (adj.) Insular: narrow-minded; or relating to an island.

#### Example:
- (n.) The *insularity* of the small town made strangers feel unwelcome.
- (n.) The company's *insularity* kept it from seeing what customers wanted.
- (adj.) The writer attacked the *insular* attitudes of some politicians.
