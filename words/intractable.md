### Intractable (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Hard to control or manage; stubborn.
- Hard to solve or fix, such as a problem.
- (medicine) Hard to treat or cure.

#### Example:
- The *intractable* mule refused to move, no matter what we did.
- Poverty remains an *intractable* problem in the region.
- (medicine) He suffered from *intractable* back pain that no drug could ease.
