### Intransigence (n.), Intransigent (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Refusal to change your opinion or to agree, even when it makes sense to do so.
- (adj.) Intransigent: stubborn in this way.

#### Example:
- (n.) The union's *intransigence* ended the talks without a deal.
- (adj.) Both sides stayed *intransigent*, so the strike continued.
