### Invective (n.), Invective (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Harsh, insulting words used to attack someone.
- (adj.) Full of such insults; abusive.

#### Example:
- (n.) The senator hurled *invective* at his rivals during the debate.
- (n.) The comment section was full of *invective* after the game.
- (adj.) The editor refused to print the *invective* letter.
