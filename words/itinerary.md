### Itinerary (n.), Itinerary (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A plan for a trip, listing places and times.
- (n.) A record of a journey already made.
- (adj.) Traveling from place to place (rare).

#### Example:
- (n.) Our *itinerary* includes three days in Rome and two in Florence.
- (n.) His diary was a detailed *itinerary* of the trip across Asia.
- (adj.) An *itinerary* preacher visited each village once a year.
