### Laud (v.), Laud (n.), Laudable (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To praise strongly, often in public.
- (n.) Praise (formal).
- (adj.) Laudable: deserving praise.

#### Example:
- (v.) Critics *lauded* the film as a masterpiece.
- (v.) The mayor *lauded* the firefighters for their courage.
- (n.) The team received great *laud* for its work during the flood.
- (adj.) Raising money for the school was a *laudable* goal.
