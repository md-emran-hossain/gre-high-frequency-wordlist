### Lethargic (adj.), Lethargy (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Very sleepy, slow, and without energy.
- (adj.) Unwilling to act or care; passive.
- (n.) Lethargy: the state of having no energy or interest.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The big meal left us *lethargic* all afternoon.
- (adj.) A *lethargic* government was slow to answer the crisis.
- (n.) He sank into *lethargy* after losing his job.
