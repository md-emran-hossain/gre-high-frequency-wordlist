### Levee (n.), Levee (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A high wall of earth or stone built beside a river to stop floods.
- (n.) A natural raised bank beside a river, left by floods.
- (v.) To build a levee along a river.

#### Example:
- (n.) The *levee* broke and water poured into the town.
- (n.) Sand left by floods slowly built a *levee* beside the river.
- (v.) Farmers *leveed* the riverbank before the rainy season.
