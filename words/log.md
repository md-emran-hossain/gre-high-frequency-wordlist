### Log (n.), Log (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A thick piece of a tree trunk or branch, cut or fallen.
- (n.) A written record of events, such as a ship's trip or daily work.
- (n.) Short for logarithm<sup>1</sup>, a math function.
- (v.) To write something down in a record.
- (v.) To reach a total, such as hours or distance.
- (v.) To enter or leave a computer account (log in / log out).

#### Example:
- (n.) We sat on a fallen *log* by the fire.
- (n.) The captain wrote the storm in the ship's *log*.
- (n.) The *log* of 100 to base 10 is 2.
- (v.) The nurse *logged* the patient's temperature every hour.
- (v.) The pilot has *logged* over ten thousand flying hours.
- (v.) Please *log* in with your password.

##### Unknown words:
1. Logarithm - The power a base number must be raised to in order to get another number.
