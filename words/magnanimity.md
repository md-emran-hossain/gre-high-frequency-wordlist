### Magnanimity (n.), Magnanimous (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) Generous spirit, especially forgiving an enemy or a rival.
- (adj.) Magnanimous: kind and generous in this way, not petty.

#### Example:
- (n.) The winner showed *magnanimity* by praising the loser.
- (adj.) The boss was *magnanimous* and forgave the mistake.
