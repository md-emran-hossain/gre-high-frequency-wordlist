### Malingerer (n.), Malinger (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person who pretends to be sick or hurt to avoid work or duty.
- (v.) Malinger: to pretend to be ill in this way.

#### Example:
- (n.) The sergeant called him a *malingerer* and sent him back to work.
- (v.) He *malingered* for a week to miss the exam.
