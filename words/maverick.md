### Maverick (n.), Maverick (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person who thinks and acts independently and does not follow the group.
- (adj.) Independent and not following the usual rules.
- (n.) A calf or cow with no brand<sup>1</sup> mark (original meaning, US West).

#### Example:
- (n.) The *maverick* senator often voted against his own party.
- (adj.) She is a *maverick* chef who ignores classic recipes.
- (n.) The rancher found a *maverick* in the herd and branded it.

##### Unknown words:
1. Brand - A mark burned on an animal's skin to show its owner.
