### Mendacious (adj.), Mendacity (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Not telling the truth; lying or often lying.
- (adj.) Of a statement: false.
- (n.) Mendacity: the habit of lying; a lie.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The *mendacious* witness lied about where he had been that night.
- (adj.) The newspaper printed a *mendacious* report about the accident.
- (n.) Her *mendacity* ended the friendship.
