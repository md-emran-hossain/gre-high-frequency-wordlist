### Metamorphosis (n.), Metamorphose (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A complete change of form, such as a caterpillar becoming a butterfly.
- (n.) A big change in someone's character, look, or situation.
- (v.) Metamorphose: to change completely.

#### Example:
- (n.) The *metamorphosis* of a tadpole into a frog takes several weeks.
- (n.) The city has gone through a *metamorphosis* since the new factory opened.
- (v.) The caterpillar *metamorphosed* inside its cocoon.
