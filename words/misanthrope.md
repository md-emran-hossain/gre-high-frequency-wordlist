### Misanthrope (n.), Misanthropic (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person who hates or distrusts people in general and avoids them.
- (adj.) Misanthropic: hating or distrusting people.

#### Example:
- (n.) The old *misanthrope* lived alone and never answered the door.
- (adj.) Her *misanthropic* jokes made the guests uneasy.
