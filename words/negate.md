### Negate (v.), Negation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To cancel the effect of something; to make it useless.
- (v.) To say or show that something is not true; to deny.
- (n.) Negation: the act of denying, or the opposite of something.

#### Example:
- (v.) Late fees can *negate* the discount.
- (v.) The new evidence *negates* his claim.
- (n.) Her calm tone was the *negation* of his anger.
