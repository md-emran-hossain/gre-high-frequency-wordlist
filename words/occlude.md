### Occlude (v.), Occlusion (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To block or close an opening, such as a tube or blood vessel.
- (v.) To hide or cover something from view.
- (dentistry) To close, as the upper and lower teeth meet.
- (n.) Occlusion: a blockage.

#### Example:
- (v.) Fat can *occlude* an artery<sup>1</sup> and cause a heart attack.
- (v.) Thick clouds *occluded* the sun all morning.
- (dentistry) The dentist checked how his teeth *occlude* when he bites down.
- (n.) The scan showed an *occlusion* in a blood vessel.

##### Unknown words:
1. Artery - A tube that carries blood from the heart to the body.
