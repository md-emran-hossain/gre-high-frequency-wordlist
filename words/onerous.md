### Onerous (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- Hard to bear; heavy and tiring, as a duty or task.
- (law) Costing more than the benefit it gives.

#### Example:
- Caring for three sick relatives was an *onerous* duty.
- The new reporting rules are *onerous* for small shops.
- (law) The contract had *onerous* terms, so the buyer lost money on the deal.
