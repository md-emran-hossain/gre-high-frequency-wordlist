### Oscillate (v.), Oscillation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To swing back and forth regularly, like a pendulum.
- (v.) To keep changing between two opinions, moods, or states.
- (n.) Oscillation: such a movement or change.

#### Example:
- (v.) The desk fan *oscillates* to cool the whole room.
- (v.) Voters *oscillated* between the two candidates until election day.
- (n.) Prices showed wild *oscillations* all week.
