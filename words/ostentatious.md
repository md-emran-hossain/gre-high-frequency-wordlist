### Ostentatious (adj.), Ostentation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Showing wealth or skill in a way meant to impress others; too showy.
- (n.) Ostentation: such showing off.

#### Example:
- (adj.) He drove an *ostentatious* gold car to the office.
- (adj.) The wedding was big and *ostentatious*, with a hundred white doves.
- (n.) He gave the money with great *ostentation* so everyone would notice.
