### Paragon (n.), Paragon (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A perfect example of a good quality; a model.
- (n.) A perfect diamond of 100 carats or more.
- (v.) To compare with or equal (old, rare).

#### Example:
- (n.) The nurse was a *paragon* of patience with every patient.
- (n.) The museum displayed a *paragon* the size of a walnut.
- (v.) No other town could *paragon* its beauty.
