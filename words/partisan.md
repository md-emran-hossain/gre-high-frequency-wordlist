### Partisan (adj.), Partisan (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Strongly supporting one side, often unfairly.
- (n.) A strong supporter of a person, group, or cause.
- (n.) A member of a secret fighting group resisting an enemy army that holds their land.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The debate was too *partisan* to be useful.
- (n.) He is a loyal *partisan* of the president.
- (n.) *Partisans* blew up the railway bridge behind enemy lines.
