### Pathological (adj.), Pathology (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (medicine) Caused by or relating to disease.
- (adj.) Extreme and unreasonable, as if it were an illness.
- (n.) Pathology: the study of diseases.

#### Example:
- (medicine) The lab report said the cells showed *pathological* changes.
- (adj.) He is a *pathological* liar who lies even when there is no reason.
- (n.) She works in a hospital lab that does *pathology*.
