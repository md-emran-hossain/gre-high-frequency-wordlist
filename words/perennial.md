### Perennial (adj.), Perennial (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Lasting a very long time, or returning again and again.
- (adj.) Of a plant: living for more than two years.
- (n.) A plant that lives for many years and flowers each year.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Traffic is a *perennial* problem in this city.
- (adj.) Lavender is a *perennial* plant, so it grows back every spring.
- (n.) She planted *perennials* so the garden would return every year.
