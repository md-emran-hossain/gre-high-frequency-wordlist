### Plummet (v.), Plummet (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To fall very fast and far.
- (v.) Of prices, numbers, or feelings: to drop suddenly and strongly.
- (n.) A sudden, dramatic fall.

#### Example:
- (v.) The small plane *plummeted* into the sea.
- (v.) Stock prices *plummeted* after the company lost its biggest client.
- (n.) The *plummet* in home sales worried the banks.
