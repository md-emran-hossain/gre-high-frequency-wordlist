### Pragmatic (adj.), Pragmatist (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Dealing with things in a practical way, based on what works.
- (n.) Pragmatist: a practical person.

#### Example:
- (adj.) She took a *pragmatic* approach and fixed the problem fast.
- (n.) He is a *pragmatist* who cares about results, not about ideas.
