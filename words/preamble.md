### Preamble (n.), Preamble (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) An introduction at the start of a speech, law, or document.
- (n.) Something that comes before and leads to something else.
- (v.) To make an introduction (rare).

#### Example:
- (n.) The *preamble* of the constitution begins with "We the People".
- (n.) The quarrel was a *preamble* to war.
- (v.) The speaker *preambled* for ten minutes before making his point.
