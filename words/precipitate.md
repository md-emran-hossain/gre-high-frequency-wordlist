### Precipitate (v.), Precipitate (adj.), Precipitate (n.), Precipitation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To make something happen suddenly or sooner than expected.
- (v.) To throw down from a great height.
- (chemistry) To form solid bits that separate out of a liquid.
- (weather) To fall to the ground as rain, snow or hail.
- (adj.) Done too fast, without enough thought.
- (n.) The solid that forms in a liquid during a chemical reaction.
- Precipitation (n.): rain, snow or hail.

#### Example:
- (v.) The bank's collapse *precipitated* a crisis across the country.
- (v.) The strong wind *precipitated* the climber down the slope.
- (chemistry) Silver *precipitates* as a white solid when salt is added to the solution.
- (weather) Moisture in the clouds *precipitates* as snow in winter.
- (adj.) Quitting his job was a *precipitate* decision, and he soon regretted it.
- (n.) A white *precipitate* settled at the bottom of the test tube.
- Precipitation: Heavy *precipitation* is expected over the weekend.
