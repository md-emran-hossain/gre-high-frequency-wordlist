### Precursor (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Something or someone that came before and led to a later one.
- A sign that something is coming.
- (chemistry) A substance that turns into another substance.

#### Example:
- The telegraph was a *precursor* of the telephone.
- Dark clouds are often the *precursor* of a storm.
- (chemistry) This chemical is a *precursor* in making the medicine.
