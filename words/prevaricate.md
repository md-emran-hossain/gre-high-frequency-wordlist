### Prevaricate (v.), Prevarication (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To avoid giving a clear or honest answer.
- Prevarication (n.): speech that avoids the truth.

#### Example:
- The minister *prevaricated* when reporters asked where the money went.
- The witness *prevaricated* under questioning in court.
- Prevarication: His constant *prevarication* made us doubt him.
