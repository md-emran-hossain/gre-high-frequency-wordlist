### Prodigal (adj.), Prodigal (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Spending money in a careless, wasteful way.
- (adj.) Giving very freely and in large amounts.
- (n.) A person who wastes money, or who leaves home and later returns.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The *prodigal* heir spent his fortune in a single year.
- (adj.) She was *prodigal* with her time and helped every student.
- (n.) The *prodigal* came home after years abroad, and his family welcomed him.
