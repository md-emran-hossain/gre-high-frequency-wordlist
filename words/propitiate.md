### Propitiate (v.), Propitious (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- To calm an angry person or god with a gift or kind act.
- Propitious (adj.): favorable; a good sign for the future.

#### Example:
- The villagers made offerings to *propitiate* the gods of the river.
- He wrote an apology to *propitiate* his angry boss.
- Propitious: It was a *propitious* time to open a new shop.
