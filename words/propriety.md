### Propriety (n.), Proprieties (n.)
#### Meaning:
- Correct, polite behavior that follows social rules.
- The quality of being suitable or right.
- Proprieties (n.): the rules of polite behavior.

#### Example:
- She behaved with perfect *propriety* at the formal dinner.
- Reporters questioned the *propriety* of the mayor's gift from a contractor.
- Proprieties: He always followed the *proprieties* when meeting clients.
