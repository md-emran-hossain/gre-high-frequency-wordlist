### Proscribe (v.), Proscription (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To forbid by law or rule.
- To condemn or reject something.
- Proscription (n.): an official ban.

#### Example:
- The government *proscribed* the group as a terrorist organization.
- Exam rules *proscribe* phones in the test room.
- Many religions *proscribe* certain foods.
- Proscription: The *proscription* of alcohol lasted thirteen years in the US.
