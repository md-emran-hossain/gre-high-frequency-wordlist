### Qualified (adj.), Qualify (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Having the training or skills needed for a job.
- (adj.) Limited; with conditions attached.
- (v.) To become allowed or able to take part in something.
- (v.) To make a statement weaker by adding conditions.

#### Example:
- (adj.) She is a *qualified* nurse with ten years of experience.
- (adj.) The manager gave the plan only *qualified* approval.
- (v.) Our team *qualified* for the final after winning three games.
- (v.) I must *qualify* my remark: it is true only in some cases.
