### Quibble (n.), Quibble (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A small, unimportant complaint.
- (v.) To argue about something small and unimportant.

#### Example:
- (n.) I have only one *quibble* with your essay: the title is too long.
- (v.) Let's not *quibble* over a few dollars.
- (v.) They *quibbled* for an hour about where to put a comma.
