### Rarefied (adj.), Rarefy (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Thin, with less air, like high on a mountain.
- (adj.) Known or enjoyed only by a small, special group.
- (v.) To make air or gas thinner.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Climbers struggle to breathe in the *rarefied* air near the summit.
- (adj.) He lives in the *rarefied* world of the super-rich.
- (v.) Heating a gas can *rarefy* it as it expands.
