### Recalcitrant (adj.), Recalcitrant (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Stubbornly refusing to obey.
- (adj.) Hard to control or deal with.
- (n.) A person who stubbornly resists orders.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The teacher struggled with a *recalcitrant* class.
- (adj.) The old engine was *recalcitrant* and would not start in the cold.
- (n.) The company warned the *recalcitrants* who ignored the new policy.
