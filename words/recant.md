### Recant (v.), Recantation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To say publicly that something you said or believed was wrong.
- Recantation (n.): the act of recanting.

#### Example:
- Under pressure from the church, the scientist was forced to *recant* his theory.
- The witness *recanted* her statement the next day.
- Recantation: The newspaper printed a *recantation* of its false story.
