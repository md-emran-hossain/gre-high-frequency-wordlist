### Refractory (adj.), Refractory (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Stubborn and hard to control.
- (medicine) Not responding to treatment.
- (adj.) Able to stand very high heat without melting.
- (n.) A heat-resistant material used to line furnaces.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The *refractory* child refused to eat, sleep, or listen.
- (medicine) Her cough was *refractory* and did not improve with any medicine.
- (adj.) Furnaces are lined with *refractory* bricks that survive extreme heat.
- (n.) The factory makes *refractories* for steel plants.
