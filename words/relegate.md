### Relegate (v.), Relegation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To move someone or something to a lower, less important position.
- (sports) To send a team down to a lower league<sup>1</sup>.
- To hand a task over to someone else.

#### Example:
- Old computers were *relegated* to the storage room.
- (sports) The club was *relegated* after losing its last match.
- The manager *relegated* the small details to his assistant.

##### Unknown words:
1. League - A group of sports teams that play each other.
