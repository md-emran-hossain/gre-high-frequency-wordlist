### Reproach (v.), Reproach (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To tell someone you are disappointed in what they did.
- (n.) Words or a look showing disappointment or blame.
- (n.) Something that brings shame.

#### Example:
- (v.) She *reproached* her son for coming home so late.
- (n.) His eyes were full of *reproach* when I broke my promise.
- (n.) The poor state of the hospital is a *reproach* to the government.
