### Reprobate (n.), Reprobate (adj.), Reprobate (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person with bad morals who feels no shame.
- (adj.) Morally bad and shameless.
- (v.) To strongly condemn<sup>1</sup> (old use).

#### Example:
- (n.) The old *reprobate* spent his life gambling.
- (adj.) The preacher called the town a *reprobate* place.
- (v.) The church *reprobated* the sale of such books.

##### Unknown words:
1. Condemn - To say strongly that something is wrong.
