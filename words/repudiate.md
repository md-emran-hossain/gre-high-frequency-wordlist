### Repudiate (v.), Repudiation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To reject something as untrue or unfair.
- To refuse to accept a debt, agreement or belief.
- To cut ties with someone.

#### Example:
- The senator *repudiated* the report as false.
- The new government *repudiated* the debts of the old one.
- He *repudiated* his son after the lie.
