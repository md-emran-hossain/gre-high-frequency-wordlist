### Resolution (n.)
#### Meaning:
- A firm decision to do or not do something.
- Strong determination.
- The solving of a problem or dispute.
- A formal decision voted on by a group.
- The amount of detail in an image or screen.

#### Example:
- My New Year's *resolution* is to read one book a week.
- She faced the crisis with great *resolution*.
- The two countries reached a peaceful *resolution* of the border dispute.
- The UN passed a *resolution* against the attack.
- This phone camera has a higher *resolution* than my old one.
