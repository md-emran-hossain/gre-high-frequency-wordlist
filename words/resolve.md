### Resolve (v.), Resolve (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To decide firmly to do something.
- (v.) To solve a problem or end a disagreement.
- (v.) To officially vote for something.
- (n.) Strong determination.

#### Example:
- (v.) He *resolved* to quit smoking.
- (v.) The bank *resolved* the billing error within a day.
- (v.) The committee *resolved* to build a new library.
- (n.) Her *resolve* never weakened, even after many failures.
