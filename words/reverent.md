### Reverent (adj.), Reverence (n.), Revere (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Showing deep respect, often for something holy.
- (n.) Reverence: deep respect and admiration.
- (v.) Revere: to respect and admire very much.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The crowd stood in *reverent* silence at the temple.
- (n.) They treat their old traditions with great *reverence*.
- (v.) Many people *revere* Gandhi as a moral leader.
