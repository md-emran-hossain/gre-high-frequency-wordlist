### Sage (n.), Sage (adj.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A very wise person, often old and respected.
- (adj.) Wise, showing good judgment.
- (n.) A plant with grey-green leaves used to flavor food.

#### Example:
- (n.) The villagers asked the *sage* for advice.
- (adj.) He gave me some *sage* advice about money.
- (n.) Fry the sausages with a little butter and *sage*.
