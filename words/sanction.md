### Sanction (n.), Sanction (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To give official approval.
- (n.) Official approval.
- (n.) A penalty for breaking a rule.
- (n.) Measures one country takes against another, such as stopping trade, to force a change.
- (v.) To punish.

#### Example:
- (v.) The board *sanctioned* the use of the new software.
- (n.) The plan was carried out with the full *sanction* of the mayor.
- (n.) Cheating brings a heavy *sanction* from the school.
- (n.) Many countries put economic *sanctions* on the regime<sup>1</sup>.
- (v.) The league *sanctioned* the club for breaking the rules.

##### Unknown words:
1. Regime - A government, often one that is harsh.
