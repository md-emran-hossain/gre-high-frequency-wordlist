### Saturate (v.), Saturated (adj.), Saturation (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To soak something completely with liquid.
- To fill something so full that nothing more can be added.
- To supply a market with so much of a product that more cannot be sold.

#### Example:
- The heavy rain *saturated* the ground.
- News about the election *saturates* social media.
- Cheap phones have *saturated* the market.
