### Savor (v.), Savor (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To enjoy something slowly and fully, especially food or a moment.
- (v.) To have a hint of a quality (used with "of").
- (n.) The taste or smell of something.

#### Example:
- (v.) She *savored* every bite of the chocolate cake.
- (v.) His speech *savored* of arrogance.
- (n.) The soup had a sweet *savor* of ginger.
