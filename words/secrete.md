### Secrete (v.), Secretion (n.)
#### Meaning:
- To produce and release a liquid from a gland<sup>1</sup> or cell.
- To hide something in a secret place.

#### Example:
- The pancreas *secretes* insulin.
- The thief *secreted* the jewels under the floor.

##### Unknown words:
1. Gland - A body part that makes substances such as sweat or hormones.
