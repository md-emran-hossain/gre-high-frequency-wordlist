### Spectrum (n.)
#### Meaning:
- The band of colors made when white light is split, as in a rainbow.
- A wide range of different things between two extremes.
- The range of waves of one kind, such as radio waves.

#### Example:
- A rainbow shows the *spectrum* of visible light.
- The party has members from across the political *spectrum*.
- Radio stations must share a limited *spectrum* of waves.
