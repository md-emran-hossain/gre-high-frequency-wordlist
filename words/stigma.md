### Stigma (n.), Stigmatize (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A mark of shame; a bad opinion society has of a person or group.
- (n.) (botany) The part of a flower that receives pollen<sup>1</sup>.
- (v.) To treat someone as shameful or unworthy.

#### Example:
- (n.) There is still a *stigma* attached to mental illness.
- (n.) (botany) Insects carry pollen to the *stigma* of the flower.
- (v.) The media *stigmatized* the whole village as criminals.

##### Unknown words:
1. Pollen - Fine powder made by flowers.
