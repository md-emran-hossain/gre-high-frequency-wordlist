### Stint (v.), Stint (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (v.) To give or use too little of something.
- (n.) A fixed period of time doing a job or activity.

#### Example:
- (v.) Parents should not *stint* on their children's education.
- (v.) Never *stint* on safety equipment.
- (n.) He did a two-year *stint* in the army.
