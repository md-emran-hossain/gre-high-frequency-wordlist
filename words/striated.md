### Striated (adj.), Striation (n.), Striate (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Marked with thin parallel lines or bands.
- (v.) To mark with such lines.

#### Example:
- (adj.) The cliff has *striated* layers of red and grey rock.
- (v.) Glaciers<sup>1</sup> *striate* the rocks they pass over.

##### Unknown words:
1. Glacier - A huge, slow river of ice.
