### Transgression (n.), Transgress (v.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) The breaking of a law, rule or moral code.
- (n.) A sin or wrongdoing.
- (v.) To go beyond a limit or break a rule.

#### Example:
- (n.) Parking here is a minor *transgression* of the rules.
- (n.) He was forgiven for his youthful *transgressions*.
- (v.) Do not *transgress* the limits of good taste.
