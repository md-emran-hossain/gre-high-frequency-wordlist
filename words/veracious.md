### Veracious (adj.), Veracity (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Always telling the truth.
- (adj.) Accurate and true.
- (n.) Veracity: truth or accuracy.

#### Example:
- (adj.) He is a *veracious* witness who never exaggerates.
- (adj.) Journalists must give a *veracious* report of events.
- (n.) I doubt the *veracity* of his story.
