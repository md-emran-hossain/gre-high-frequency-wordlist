### Volatile (adj.), Volatile (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Changing quickly and often; unstable.
- (adj.) Likely to become violent.
- (adj.) (of a liquid) Quickly turning into gas.
- (n.) A substance that turns into gas easily.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Oil prices are *volatile* this year.
- (adj.) The situation in the city is *volatile* after the protests.
- (adj.) Petrol is highly *volatile* and catches fire easily.
- (n.) Alcohol is a *volatile* that dries fast on the skin.
