### Warranted (adj.), Warrant (v.), Warrant (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (adj.) Justified; having a good reason.
- (v.) To make something seem right or necessary.
- (v.) To promise the quality of a product.
- (n.) A legal paper that lets police arrest or search.

#### Example:
- (adj.) Her fear was *warranted*, given the dark street.
- (v.) The crime *warrants* a harsh punishment.
- (v.) The shop *warrants* this watch for two years.
- (n.) The police got a *warrant* to search the house.
