### Zealot (n.), Zealous (adj.), Zeal (n.)
#### Meaning:
- (n.) A person extremely devoted to a cause.
- (adj.) Full of energy and eagerness.
- (n.) Zeal: great enthusiasm for a cause.

#### Example:
- (n.) The religious *zealot* refused to listen to any other view.
- (adj.) The *zealous* volunteers cleaned the whole beach.
- (n.) She worked with *zeal* to finish the project.
